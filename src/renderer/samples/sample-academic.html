🎓 Neural Architecture Search via Reinforcement Learning

Authors: Dr. Alex Mercer, Prof. Elena Rostova — Institute of Advanced AI Computing

Abstract: We propose an efficient gradient-based neural architecture search algorithm (NAS-RL) that reduces hyperparameter optimization time by 14x while maintaining high classification accuracy on benchmark datasets.

1. Introduction & Problem Formalization

Deep learning models require careful architecture design. Manual architecture engineering is labor-intensive and prone to human bias. In this paper, we formalize model selection as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) governed by policy πθ.

Let A denote the space of candidate layer configurations. The expected reward function J(θ) is defined over operational time steps t = 1 … T as:

J(θ) = E_[τ ~ πθ] [ Σ_{t=1..T} γt · R(st, at) ]

2. Mathematical Formulation & Policy Gradients

The policy gradient update with exponential moving baseline b is formulated as:

∇θ J(θ) = Σ_{t=1..T} E_[πθ] [ ∇θ log πθ(at | st) · (Rt - b) ]

Candidate operation selection probability via temperature-scaled softmax:

P(at = k | st) = exp(zk / τ) / Σ_{j=1..K} exp(zj / τ)
