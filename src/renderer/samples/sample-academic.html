🎓 Neural Architecture Search via Reinforcement Learning

Authors: Dr. Alex Mercer, Prof. Elena Rostova — Institute of Advanced AI Computing

Abstract: We propose an efficient gradient-based neural architecture search algorithm (NAS-RL) that reduces hyperparameter optimization time by 14x while maintaining high classification accuracy on benchmark datasets.

1. Introduction

Deep learning models require careful architecture design. Manual architecture engineering is labor-intensive and prone to human bias. In this paper, we formalize model selection as a Markov Decision Process (MDP).
